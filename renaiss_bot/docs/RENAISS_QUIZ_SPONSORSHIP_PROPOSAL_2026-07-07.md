## What we're building

A Telegram collector-game bot powered by the **Renaiss Index API**. Users collect free digital cards, and every card they see carries real graded-market prices (PSA/BGS/CGC slab labels rendered on card images).

We're adding a **Daily Price Quiz** to our official community group:

- Once a day, the bot posts a card: **"Guess the PSA 10 price!"** with 4 answer buttons
- After 2 minutes, the real Renaiss Index price is revealed with a slab-labeled card image and a link to the asset page on Renaiss
- Weekly leaderboard crowns the best price-guessers

Alongside the quiz, the bot ships a **portfolio tracker** (collection value with 7-day change, wallet-style) and a **grading premium guide** (`RAW $45 → PSA 10 $390, 8.7x`) — both built entirely on Index API data.

## What Renaiss gets

- **Daily organic exposure of Index data** — every quiz answer is a mini-showcase of Renaiss pricing, in front of crypto-native collectors
- **Traffic**: every price reveal links to the Renaiss asset page (referral-tracked, measurable)
- **Education funnel**: users learn grading premiums by playing — the exact mental model a future slab buyer needs
- **A live builder case study** for the Index API: a consumer product generating daily engagement from beta data

## The ask

A small weekly prize for quiz leaderboard winners:

- **Option A:** platform credits on Renaiss
- **Option B:** cash prize pool, ~$50–100/month total

We handle everything else — quiz operation is fully automated, prizes are announced in-group with Renaiss credited as sponsor.

## Data disclaimer we already follow

Index API values are presented as **experimental reference data** (beta), never as verified market facts or investment advice. This is stated in the bot and in our hackathon submission.
